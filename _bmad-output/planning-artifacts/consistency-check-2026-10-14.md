# Consistency Check: AI Study Buddy planning documents (2026-10-14)

**Scope:** PRD (`prds/prd-AI-StudyBuddy-2026-10-06/prd.md`), UX spines (`ux-designs/ux-AI-StudyBuddy-2026-10-13/DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`), the nine `mockups/key-*.html` (visible text and captions), and both `.memlog.md` files as the source of truth (later entries and `(override)` win).

## Verdict

**Mostly consistent, with cleanup needed.** All the numeric limits and the core behavior rules match across the PRD, both spines, and the memlogs. That covers Quiz, Flashcards, and Mistake Review at 20 per generation; pickers of 5/10/20 with default 10; 50 chat messages per Account per day across all chats; 25 images per day; 10 files including Reading…; 50 MB per file and per photo; topics at 3 / about 1 per 5 pages / 100; running-low at 10 or fewer; about 10 minutes for the phone link; 30-day session; 30-day purge; Quiz-only Mistake Review; soft project delete versus immediate account delete; verify-before-linking and the takeover rule. The value 50 does not appear for flashcards anywhere.

The drift is in three places:

1. **EXPERIENCE.md still describes the PRD as it was before the update.** Its "Scope Changes vs PRD" table, its IA FR column, and its flow numbers do not reflect FR-19..FR-29 and UJ-4/UJ-5.
2. **Some mocks predate the latest spine decisions.** These include the delete-project confirm button and losses box, the remove-file labels, and Norwegian wording.
3. **One real behavior conflict.** The Settings mock disables "Change password" for unverified email accounts, which contradicts PRD FR-27, FR-3, and the C4 decision.

No limit or core rule conflicts between the PRD and the spines.

**Count:** 28 inconsistencies (PRD 3, EXPERIENCE.md 9, DESIGN.md 4, mocks 12).
