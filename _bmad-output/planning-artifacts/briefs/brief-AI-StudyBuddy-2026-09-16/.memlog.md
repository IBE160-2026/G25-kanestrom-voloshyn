- (decision) Primary users: university/college students studying from digital course materials (PDFs, slides, Word docs, notes).
- (decision) Core concept: student uploads study material, then studies directly from it using AI-powered tools.
- (decision) V1 (MVP) feature set: Quiz (generate questions from selected topics), Flashcards, Summarize (whole doc or selected parts), Ask AI (freeform prompt about the material), Mistake Review (re-practice from incorrectly answered questions), Saved Projects (save/resume a study project).
- (decision) Explicitly out of V1: detailed progress tracking, adaptive learning, spaced repetition, advanced recommendations -- deferred to later versions, not MVP requirements.
- (decision) Working mode: Coaching path -- user explicitly asked to be asked questions when info is missing rather than have assumptions made for them.
- (decision) Brief constraint: keep final brief concise (~1-2 pages), focused on product purpose and user outcomes, not technical implementation.
- (decision) Problem: turning PDFs/slides/notes into quizzes, flashcards, summaries takes time; students lose track of mistakes and what to review.
- (decision) Differentiation (honest, no false novelty claim): StudyFetch, NotebookLM, Quizlet, Quizgecko, Revisely already cover parts of this workflow. V1 differentiator is simplicity/integration -- one smooth path from uploaded material to Quiz/Flashcards/Summarize/Ask AI, with Mistake Review wired directly into the same saved project. Not competing on feature count.
- (decision) Out of scope for V1 (final list): detailed progress analytics, spaced repetition, advanced adaptive learning, cross-document knowledge linking, advanced study recommendations, full exam planning.
- (decision) Success criteria (V1, functional): user can upload material; generate relevant quiz/flashcards/summary from it; ask questions about it; complete a quiz with incorrect answers remembered; review past mistakes; save and resume a project. Plus: generated content stays relevant to source material, and workflow is simple enough to use without much explanation.
- (decision) Long-term vision: personalized study system -- tracks performance by topic, identifies weak areas, adapts questions from past mistakes, adds spaced repetition, links knowledge across multiple course documents.
- (decision) Constraint: brief must not present the product as the first/only tool using AI with uploaded study material -- competitive landscape must be acknowledged honestly.
- (decision) Form factor: web app for PC/desktop.
