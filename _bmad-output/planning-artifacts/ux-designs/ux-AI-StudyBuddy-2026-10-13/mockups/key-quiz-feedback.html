During which stage do homologous chromosomes separate and move to opposite poles of the cell?

1Anaphase of mitosis
2Anaphase I of meiosisCorrect answer
3Anaphase II of meiosisYour answer
4Telophase I of meiosis

Not quite

It stays in your review.

It's Anaphase I. In meiosis I, each homologous pair lines up together at the metaphase plate, and in Anaphase I the pairs are pulled apart, one chromosome to each pole. The sister chromatids stay joined. Anaphase II looks similar, but there it's the sister chromatids that separate, just like in mitosis.
