Why does a dihybrid cross give a 9:3:3:1 ratio?

Study Buddy

Because the two genes sort independently. Each parent is heterozygous for both traits, so each makes four kinds of gametes in equal numbers. Combining them in a 4×4 Punnett square gives 16 boxes: 9 show both dominant traits, 3 and 3 show one dominant trait each, and 1 shows both recessive traits.
